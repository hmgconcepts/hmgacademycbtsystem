📜 Site License & Subscription

Dual-engine licensing: an offline perpetual token (cryptographic, no phone-home) and a subscription lifecycle (lifetime or billed cycles with grace days). The subscription state is re-evaluated on every page, re-checked every 15 minutes, and after expiry + grace it locks the portal with a renewal screen.

🔑 Subscription Manager super_admin only

Proprietor/owner console. Changes are written through the owner-checked save_site_license() RPC, signed for tamper evidence, and audit-logged.

Quick-extend uses the owner RPC extend_site_license() — one click after a renewal payment is received.

🔐 Offline Perpetual Token Engine

A cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 certificate that verifies completely offline — no phone-home, no server. Generate a token for this institution and keep it with the ownership documents.

Registry (optional, authoritative)

A remote JSON on hosting the client does not control. When configured, its verdict overrides local rows — local edits cannot suspend or extend terms. Managed in Settings → platform_settings.license_registry_url.
